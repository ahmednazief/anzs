Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2026

At Cuttify, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how Cuttify (the "Software"), developed by Ahmed Nazif ("we", "us", or "our"), processes information, and explains the data collection practices of our website (ahmednazif.com/cuttify).

Core Privacy Principle: Local-First Processing
Cuttify operates entirely locally on your workstation. The video sequences, audio tracks, editing timelines, voice recordings, and transcriptions you process do not leave your computer. We do not upload, stream, or store your creative media assets on remote cloud servers.

1. Information Processed by the Software Extension

The Cuttify Premiere Pro plugin operates offline to secure your production environment. The specific processing guidelines are as follows:

  • AI Silence Cutter and Frame Decimator: These functions use local processing engines to scan waveform changes and duplicate frames directly on your Premiere Pro timeline. No remote servers are contacted.
  • Auto-Captions: Transcription is performed entirely offline on your computer using a local neural network model (Nile-Chat 4B model). Audio speech inputs are processed locally and are not transmitted to any API or external cloud service.
  • Updates Checking: The Software contains an auto-updater mechanism that checks the remote file updater.json hosted on our server to notify you of newer releases. This check is anonymous, transmits no device identifiers, and records no telemetry about your usage habits or editing timelines.

2. Information Collected on Our Website

When you visit our website, download the free version, or buy Cuttify Pro, some limited interaction logs are collected:

  • Web Analytics: We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to monitor anonymous traffic patterns, click rates on download buttons, and page performance. This logs standard browser data, referral URLs, and approximate location details, which helps us optimize page responsiveness.
  • Payment and Licensing Data: When you purchase Cuttify Pro, the checkout flow is handled securely by our payment processor, Payhip. Payhip collects your name, email address, country, billing details, and payment markers (from PayPal or Stripe). We never see or store your credit card details. This billing info is used solely to generate your license and handle transaction confirmations.

3. Cookies

Our website uses basic functional and analytical cookies to remember user sessions, track referral links, and power anonymized web statistics. You can configure your browser to reject cookies, though some web features (like payment checkouts) may require them to function correctly.

4. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party services to complete business operations. These processors are contractually bound to safeguard your data:

  • Payhip: Facilitates checkout operations and records license records.
  • Stripe & PayPal: Process secure credit card payments and money transfers.
  • Google Analytics: Provides website engagement analysis.

5. Data Retention and Security

We do not collect creative user files, so we have no video or audio data to secure or retain. For website purchase records (emails and licensing identifiers), we apply industry-standard security safeguards. License records are retained to maintain support for updates and handle potential troubleshooting requests.

6. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location (such as under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the personal billing data we retain (e.g., your email and transaction logs). If you want to request data deletion, modify licensing records, or ask questions about our data practices, please email us directly.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact Ahmed Nazif at: ahmednazief@gmail.com.
