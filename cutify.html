What It Does

Built for Speed

Every tool in Cutify is designed to cut your editing time in half without sacrificing quality.

Smart Auto-Cuts

Intelligently detects silence, dead air, and low-energy moments to remove them in one click. Keeps only the action.

Beat Sync Engine

Analyzes your background music and automatically snaps your cuts to the beat. Instant rhythmic editing.

Auto-Subtitles

Transcribes speech and generates styled animated subtitle sequences — ready to customize or export instantly.

One-Click Exports

Pre-configured export presets for YouTube, TikTok, Reels, and Discord — perfectly optimized every time.

LUT Packs Included

Ship with a curated library of cinematic LUTs tuned for gaming footage — apply any grade in one click.

Motion Presets

Ready-to-use zoom punches, screen shakes, speed ramps, and comedic transitions — drag and drop.

Workflow

Three Steps to Done

From raw footage to finished video in a fraction of the usual time.

01

Install the Plugin

Download and drop Cutify into your Premiere Pro extensions folder. Instant setup, no accounts required.

02

Choose Your Tools

Select from auto-cut, beat sync, subtitles, or all three. Tune the sensitivity sliders to your preference.

03

Apply & Export

Hit Apply. Cutify processes your timeline, makes the cuts, and queues your export in the right format.
