Workflow

Up & Running in Minutes

From install to your first automated cut in under 5 minutes.

01

Install the Plugin

Download Cutify and install it into Premiere Pro. The plugin appears in your Extensions panel — no account required.

02

Pick Your Tool

Open the Cutify panel inside Premiere Pro. Choose Remove Silences or Frame Decimation from the sidebar.

03

Apply & Done

Configure your settings, hit Analyze, then Apply. Cutify makes the cuts directly in your timeline. That's it.
