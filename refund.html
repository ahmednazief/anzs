Refund Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2026

Thank you for choosing Cuttify. We want you to have the absolute best editing experience. This Refund Policy describes the terms and options available for refunds of your Cuttify Pro purchase.

1. Try-Before-You-Buy Philosophy

Because every video editing setup is unique, we release a fully functioning Cuttify Free Edition containing our core tools (including the AI Silence Cutter and Frame Decimator). We strongly encourage you to download and test Cuttify Free on your system to verify Premiere Pro stability and offline performance before upgrading to Cuttify Pro.

2. Standard Refund Conditions

Since Cuttify Pro is a downloadable digital product that is instantly delivered and activated upon purchase, we generally do not offer automatic, unconditional refunds. Once a digital license is issued, the sale is considered final.

3. Our 14-Day Technical Guarantee

We stand behind the engineering of Cuttify. If you experience technical issues, compatibility conflicts, or software errors that prevent Cuttify Pro from functioning properly on your system, we want to help. If you meet the following conditions, we will issue a full refund:

  • You request a refund within 14 calendar days of your purchase.
  • You reach out to our support team and provide basic technical details (e.g., your Operating System, Adobe Premiere Pro version, and a brief description or screenshot of the issue) so we can troubleshoot.
  • We are unable to resolve the technical bug or provide a working solution or patch within 7 days of your support email.

If we cannot make Cuttify Pro work on your system under these circumstances, we will promptly refund the full purchase price of $14.99 USD.

4. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds will not be issued in cases where:

  • You simply change your mind, find you no longer need the tool, or do not use it.
  • You are unable to use Cuttify because your system fails to meet the minimum requirements clearly listed on our homepage (e.g., you do not have Adobe Premiere Pro 2022 or newer, or are not running Windows 10/11).
  • Your purchase was made more than 14 days ago.

5. How to Initiate a Request

To request a refund under our Technical Guarantee, please email us at ahmednazief@gmail.com with the subject line "Cuttify Pro Refund Request". Please include:

  1. Your purchase invoice or order ID from Payhip.
  2. The email address used during checkout.
  3. A description of the technical issue you encountered.

Once approved, your refund will be processed, and a credit will automatically be applied to your original payment method (PayPal or Credit Card via Stripe) through Payhip. Additionally, your Cuttify Pro license key will be deactivated.
